# SIH26229 — Kabadiwala Connect

**Ministry:** Ministry of Mines  
**Category:** Software  
**Theme:** Clean & Green Technology

## Proposed solution
Kabadiwala Connect brings the informal collector into a transparent digital recycling chain. It creates a trusted workflow for request creation, collector verification, location-aware matching, material categorisation, weighing, pricing, payment records, and recycler handoff.

## Impact
- Better earning visibility for informal collectors
- Convenient household recycling pickups
- Higher material recovery and segregation
- Traceability from pickup to recycler
- Data for municipalities/recyclers to understand collection patterns

## Differentiators
- Designed around informal collectors rather than replacing them
- Lightweight mobile-first workflow
- Transparent material pricing and weight records
- Chain-of-custody events for downstream recycling
- Analytics-ready data model
